    Questions people ask first

    How long does it take to build and publish an Android app?

    It depends on the scope. A focused first version is often a matter of weeks, and we give you a timeline once we have talked through your idea. Google's review and testing requirements for new developer accounts can add time, so we plan for them early.

    Do I need my own Google Play developer account?

    Yes. We set it up with you so the app is published under your name or company and stays yours. Google charges its own one-time registration fee, which is separate from our services.

    Are the resume templates friendly to applicant tracking systems?

    Yes. Templates use real selectable text, standard section headings and a simple reading order, with no text hidden inside images. That keeps them readable for both recruiters and software.

    Can I download my resume as a PDF?

    Use Save as PDF in the resume builder above. It opens your browser's print dialog, where you choose Save as PDF as the destination.

    Is there a live version I can try?

    Yes. The full Resume2win app runs at resume2win.onrender.com, where you can upload a resume, run an ATS check and match it to a job description. It may take a few seconds to wake up on the first visit.

    Do you build for iOS or the web too?

    We focus on Android and web projects. If you also need iOS or a cross-platform app, tell us about it and we will say what is realistic.
