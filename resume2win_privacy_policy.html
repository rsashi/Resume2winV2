This Privacy Policy explains how Resume2win ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application.

Audio is processed only to provide the feature you initiate. If audio is transmitted to an external service for speech recognition, transcription, translation, or other processing, it will be handled according to that service's privacy policy and data practices.
